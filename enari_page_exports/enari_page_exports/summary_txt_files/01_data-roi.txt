Overview
The page presents Enari's Data ROI Pit Stop as a compact consulting format for companies that need clarity before investing in a data initiative. The central business questions are whether a planned data project is technically feasible and whether the expected value justifies the investment.

Main message
The offer is positioned as a fast decision-support process. Enari helps clients evaluate business goals, existing data, technical feasibility, ROI potential, and next steps before committing to a larger implementation project.
