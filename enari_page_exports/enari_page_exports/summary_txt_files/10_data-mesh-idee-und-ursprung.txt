Overview
This article explains the origin of the Data Mesh concept and why it emerged as a response to limitations in centralized data architectures.

Main message
Traditional centralized data governance and monolithic data platforms can become bottlenecks as organizations grow. Data Mesh shifts ownership to domain teams and treats data as a product to improve scalability, agility, and trust.

Core sections
- Origin: Data Mesh is linked to Zhamak Dehghani and her work on moving beyond monolithic data lakes.
- Problems: scalability limits, bottlenecks, data quality and trust issues, insufficient empowerment of domain teams.
- Solutions: decentralized ownership, data as product, self-serve infrastructure, federated computational governance.
- Challenges: coordination, cultural change, consistent standards, implementation complexity.
- Enari positioning: Enari can support design and implementation of Data Mesh concepts.
