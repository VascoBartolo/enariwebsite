Overview
This article explains the role of data products inside a Data Mesh architecture. It covers definitions, design principles, lifecycle, technology considerations, business benefits, and use cases.

Main message
Data products are the central building blocks of Data Mesh. They turn raw data into curated, reusable, governed, user-oriented resources that domain teams own and improve over time.

Core sections
- Data products enable decentralized data management and analytics.
- Characteristics: curated datasets, findability, accessibility, security, usability, metadata, quality and governance standards.
- Difference from traditional assets: designed for user needs and reuse rather than isolated storage.
- Role in Data Mesh: supports decentralization, domain-oriented design, ownership of quality and utility.
- Lifecycle: creation, publication, maintenance, monitoring, and iteration.
- Technology: catalog systems, data platforms, governance/security, pipelines and quality controls.

Content themes
Data products, Data Mesh, domain ownership, metadata, governance, quality, discoverability, data lifecycle, reusable datasets.
